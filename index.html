Selected Work

Replacing a closed CRM with a modern Customer Data Platform

Problem: Providence relied on a closed, proprietary Healthgrades CRM with no campaign-level visibility.

What I did: Led requirements and use-case definition for the RedPoint CDP launch across email, SMS, and direct mail; grew it beyond MVP with new integrations.

Impact: Gave marketing and analytics teams direct, campaign-level data for the first time, powering hundreds of campaigns a year.

Building campaign-level attribution from scratch

Problem: No unified way to attribute performance across channels.

What I did: Built "Lamplighter," an in-house tool for UTM-tagged URLs and phone logging; led the switch to Invoca for call tracking with real-time Dynamic Number Insertion.

Impact: Granular attribution across 150+ campaigns spanning email, SMS, direct mail, social, and paid search — lower cost per campaign, better spend decisions.

Standing up a MarTech product team and Agile practice

Problem: A brand-new MarTech team with no process to build on.

What I did: Introduced Agile from the ground up — sprint cadences, documentation, stakeholder communication — and grew the team to five (product owners, a TPM, a scrum master).

Impact: Sustained delivery quality through scale-up; drove adoption of new platform capabilities across analytics, marketing, and automation teams.
